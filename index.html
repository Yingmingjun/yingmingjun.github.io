Research

Wireless Digital Twins & Ray Tracing

HoRAMA: Material-Aware 3D Reconstruction for Wireless Ray Tracing

Sept. 2024 – Present · with Prof. Rappaport and Prof. David Fouhey
  • Automated framework that turns RGB video into ray-tracing-ready 3D models with electromagnetic material labels, cutting reconstruction time from months to hours.
  • MASt3R-SLAM point clouds, PTv3 semantic segmentation, and Qwen3-VL material labeling.
  • Validated at 6.75 and 16.95 GHz over 12 TX-RX links in a 700 m² factory with NYURay; RMSE comparable to a hand-built model.
[Paper (ICC 2026)] [Poster]

NYURay: Calibrated Ray Tracer for Site-Specific Channel Prediction

Sept. 2024 – Present · with Prof. Rappaport
  • Python ray tracer with reflection, diffraction, penetration, and scattering for upper mid-band channel prediction.
  • GPU acceleration (100× speedup): 106 rays in 3–5 s per TX-RX pair; 3D tools for TX-RX placement and antenna patterns.
  • Site-specific location calibration by power-delay-profile alignment, and SPARC, a sparse path-aware residual calibrator that corrects per-path power errors from a small set of measurements.
[npj Wireless Technology 2026] [SPARC (GLOBECOM 2026)] [Location optimization (GLOBECOM 2025)]

RIS-Assisted Outdoor-to-Indoor Coverage at Upper Mid-Band

Apr. 2026 – Present · with Prof. Rappaport
  • Joint optimization of BS precoding, RIS phase shifts, and RIS deployment position to maximize the worst-user rate in a multi-user MISO outdoor-to-indoor downlink.
  • GPU-parallel solver (PRIMO) evaluated with dual-band ray tracing of the NYU MetroTech campus at FR1(C) and FR3.

Energy Efficiency & Information Theory

Waste Factor: A New Metric for Power Efficiency in Any Cascade

Jan. 2023 – Present · with Prof. Rappaport · GLOBECOM 2023 Best Paper Award
  • Waste Factor quantifies wasted power in any cascaded system: \(W = W_N + \frac{W_{N-1} - 1}{G_N} + \cdots + \frac{W_1 - 1}{\prod_{i=2}^N G_i}\).
  • Extended to MIMO, radio access networks, and data centers (PUE) to guide energy-efficient network design.
[GLOBECOM 2023] [OJ-COMS 2024] [MISO/MIMO (GLOBECOM 2024)]

Capacity of a Binary Channel with a Time-Bounded Adversary

Feb. 2024 – Present · with Prof. Sundeep Rangan and Prof. Elza Erkip
  • Derived worst-case adversarial capacity for binary channels with a time-bounded adversary.
  • Achieved it with a random interleaver and thresholded LLR decoder, compatible with 5G NR LDPC decoders.
[Paper (Asilomar 2024)] [Code]
